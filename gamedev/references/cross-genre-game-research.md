# Cross-genre game interface research for original websites

Reviewed 2026-10-19. This expansion adds **20 exact-edition profiles**, **61 analytical observations**, **55 reviewed image sources** and **one inspected video**. Nineteen added profiles have visual studies; original Dreamcast Phantasy Star Online remains a text-only research gap. The 55 images include **three production references and four capture/promotional composites**, each counted once. They are not 55 separate runtime screens.

The complete library now has **44 game/edition profiles**, with reviewed interface observations for **38**, one further atmosphere-only study, and five profiles without visual observations. It contains **107 reviewed image sources**, **nine inspected clips**, **128 visual observations**, and **24 original website interaction proposals**. These counts describe saved research, not an exhaustive game catalog or model training.

The earlier fighting/mobile research is in [modern-game-research.md](modern-game-research.md). Full evidence is searchable in the SQLite library and portable in data/games.json. Nathan Hor and Quipus are optional credited website examples. Mobbin/Mobin and CodePen are excluded.

## Findings that change the design decisions

### Horror: atmosphere can live in the control’s material

[Silent Hill 2 (original generation)](https://www.silenthillmemories.net/sh2/playing_the_game_en.htm) separates a floating object shelf, blue corner reticles, rough serif labels and a small status viewport. Its red memory list and large distressed title treatment use other compositions. [Silent Hill 2 (2024 remake)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vJje1U28BpQ) uses a red mist-like active band over a decayed scene in the viewed preview. The original and remake are separate visual dialects, not one interchangeable horror skin.

[Alien: Isolation](https://www.feralinteractive.com/en/manuals/alienisolation/latest/linux/) uses sparse radial ticks and open brackets rather than solid wedges. Its map uses fine grids and technical room outlines. The [UI designer’s interview](https://www.artofthetitle.com/title/alien-isolation/) distinguishes internal information overlays from physical terminals, and describes a material process for aged video graphics. The studied production wireframe shows individual state responsibilities; it is not runtime animation evidence.

**Original website direction:** create an archive or instrument whose labels, focus marks, imagery and feedback belong to one material. A small bracket could search for its anchor before a detail viewport resolves; a selected label could gain a low, irregular pigment field outside its text. Give its exit a different gesture. Draw the material and movement specifically for the project, and retain clear web actions. Random grain and arbitrary glitch overlays are not sufficient authorship.

### Dossiers and paperwork: operate on objects

[Batman: Arkham Asylum](https://interfaceingame.com/screenshots/batman-arkham-asylum-main-menu/) uses skewed sheets, rough portrait windows, exterior arrow marks and a separate nameplate hanging below the contact sheet. Its upgrade index, enlarged illustration and description sheet occupy distinct planes. [Papers, Please](https://www.papersplea.se/presskit/) organizes actions through a stamp rack, document placement, an irregular dotted selection contour and a nightly ledger. Object placement and edge construction carry the identity.

**Original website direction:** for a submission, project review or archive, let a real content object travel between an index, work surface and output position. A custom stamp can become a legible confirmation gesture; a projecting name strip can identify the active document. Keep the content readable before animation, and provide equally authored keyboard and touch actions. Invent the visual object and ceremony rather than turning the whole product into a game simulation.

### Rhythm and LEGO: selected states can become characters or apparatus

[Taiko no Tatsujin: Drum 'n' Fun!](https://interfaceingame.com/screenshots/taiko-no-tatsujin-drum-and-fun-select-song/) puts a detached player pin and directional marks outside its selected song/difficulty plaque; illustrated drumstick and mascot elements exceed mode-panel bounds. [LEGO Marvel Super Heroes](https://www.mobygames.com/game/62416/lego-marvel-super-heroes/screenshots/playstation-4/979840/) combines circular portrait tokens with a larger selected token and detached portrait, while customization places choices around a physical chamber.

**Original website direction:** draw a content selector as an authored emblem or small apparatus. Let an original mascot or object participate in the control’s selected silhouette; distinguish the emblem, title and directional cue so they can move separately. An exterior pin can arrive before the label settles, then tuck behind its anchor on leave. That choreography is a new proposal, not verified Taiko or LEGO animation.

### Racing: learn the geometry and the construction system

[Forza Horizon 4](https://adetheterrible.artstation.com/projects/Arb3Qz) and [Forza Horizon 5](https://williamemery.artstation.com/projects/29lJQa) show exterior focus rails, active posters projecting beyond neighboring modules, expressive wordmarks, hanging tags and separate completion art. The FH5 layout/state sheets are production guides. Their listed dimensions describe that source’s system; they are not mandatory website coordinates.

[DiRT Rally 2.0](https://taliagual.com/portfolio/dirtrally2/) uses condensed italic captions, fine selection contours, red subsection marks and an open specification field over car/service scenes. [Colin McRae Rally 2.0](https://www.mobygames.com/game/8298/colin-mcrae-rally-20/screenshots/playstation/408512/) is a contrasting classic study: tiny words form a course outline, a clipped lowercase chapter ribbon anchors the page, and technical front/rear car elevations share a selector with segmented statistics. Static captures do not prove these elements slide or rotate.

**Original website direction:** build a portfolio as a project-specific poster wall, a technical workshop, or a route atlas. Choose one coherent structural metaphor. A detached focus rail can travel independently of its image; an ownership/status ticket can hang from a seam; a technical specimen can replace a standard product card. Draw selected, unavailable, incomplete and complete states as related artwork before choosing easing.

### RPG and online worlds: separate commands from the scene they affect

[Final Fantasy VII Rebirth](https://www.square-enix.com/ffvii/en-gb/games/rebirth/battle/) separates a tactical command tray from the large scene and uses a luminous selection streak exceeding the label. [Final Fantasy XVI](https://mx.finalfantasyxvi.com/battle) contributes orbital ability structure, a detached pointing hand outside an open choice strip, a ceremonial update seal, and a situation map with its own timeline and external chapter label.

[Dragon Ball Xenoverse](https://dbx.bn-ent.net/01/system/) is studied through small publisher promotional insets, so only coarse anatomy is claimed. [Dragon Ball Xenoverse 2](https://en.bandainamcoent.eu/dragon-ball/news/new-content-coming-winter-2018-dragon-ball-xenoverse-2) provides more detailed category and skill screens: spear/rolled-ended ribbons, cloud ornament, gold selection, and a character projection outside the list. [Phantasy Star Online 2: New Genesis](https://ssl.pso2.jp/players/manual/menu/mmenu/) preserves a world behind a compact icon dock and pairs offset inventory/detail instruments. The original Dreamcast title has no viewed screenshots in this batch.

**Original website direction:** hold a reliable command index while a larger object, diagram or illustration responds on another plane. Give a preview, a focus ornament and a contextual action different spatial roles. For progression or a project history, draw relationships as an original orbit, branch or instrument rather than applying game iconography to an ordinary grid.

### Apex and sports: scene, statistics and ceremony have different roles

[Apex Legends](https://interfaceingame.com/screenshots/apex-legends-main-menu/) relates slanted tabs, diamond sockets, compact oblique portrait rows and large actors. The mode description projects out of its stack; the champion banner adds a crest outside its border. These archived captures do not establish current-season layout.

[FIFA 23](https://www.ea.com/ea-play/news/pitch-notes-fifa-23-career-deep-dive) and [EA SPORTS FC 25](https://www.ea.com/games/ea-sports-fc/fc-25/news/pitch-notes-fc-25-career-mode-deep-dive) are separate prerelease career studies. They contribute broadcast-scale typography, identity emblems, scene-backed decisions, and context actions associated with the selected scene. Their ordinary rectangular utility rows are not, by themselves, a sufficient artistic signature for an expressive art-direction brief.

**Original website direction:** compose a results or comparison page around a clear statistics spine and a separate editorial subject. A custom emblem can project above a meaningful featured state; a context action can attach to the chosen scene rather than float in a generic centered panel. Choose typography and geometry from the site’s own identity.

## Motion evidence in this expansion

[Silent Hill 2 remake demo excerpt](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vJje1U28BpQ) was paused and frame-stepped. At source positions 40.000, 40.508 and 41.525, successive pairs of footprints accumulate along an upward-right path; earlier prints remain while later prints appear. A separate square-grid indicator remains at the lower right. The complete loop and departure were not studied. Source playheads locate evidence; they are not game input latency or fitted animation durations.

The title menu and initial setup were also viewed at identified source positions. They establish visible active-state anatomy and different selected rows, not a complete measured hover/retraction sequence. Racing, rhythm, paperwork, LEGO, sports and RPG additions in this batch are still-image studies. A static production state sheet is not a transition recording.

**Original website application:** invent progress feedback from the project’s subject—registration marks, traced routes, ink impressions or mechanical positions. Show actual progress when known. Preserve the final form in reduced motion and keep indeterminate feedback distinct from a factual percentage.

## Coverage gaps that remain

- Original Dreamcast Phantasy Star Online: manual text was accessible, while image pixels were unavailable. PSO2 New Genesis was visually studied separately. “Phantasm online” remains a provisional franchise interpretation.
- Silent Hill: this batch covers original-generation SH2 and a Japanese 2024 remake demo, not all installments or the current remake patch.
- Racing: DiRT Rally 2.0 and Colin McRae Rally 2.0 were chosen explicitly. The original titled releases are distinct, unstudied editions. The DiRT loading movie did not decode.
- FIFA/FC: FIFA 23 and FC 25 are selected historical examples, not claims of current-edition coverage.
- Full pointer entry, overshoot, settle, exit, rapid re-entry and interruption sequences remain needed for the static references. Controller-selected and touch-selected artwork can inform original web hover art, but does not prove a pointer hover mechanism.

Use these gaps to choose the next focused inspection during a real website brief. Keep the bold geometry and drawing language while specifying original input states, responsive transformations and interrupted motion. The research supports developing a personal visual vocabulary, not selecting a ready-made game skin.
