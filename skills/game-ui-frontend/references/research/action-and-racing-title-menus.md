# What moves while the player decides?

In *Colin McRae Rally 2.0* a light travels around the course for several seconds while Rally stays selected. In *EA Sports FC 25* selecting Tournaments changes the screen behind the menu and the action you can take. Those movements have different jobs. A website visitor should be able to tell whether the picture is playing, their choice has changed, or they have entered a destination.

## Sunset Overdrive gives two buttons different jobs

**Waiting for a decision.** A cream-and-black diagonal runs through an oversized hand-lettered title. In its dark lower-right space, green A offers to play under the displayed profile and blue X offers to change player. At 0:26, 0:32, 0:38 and 0:45 both shortcuts occupy the same positions. Each already has an input assigned. Moving a highlight between two rows would add a step to a decision the game lets the player make directly. [PressStartOnce’s Xbox One capture, 0:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WJ0_CVUv-vA&t=26s)

**After the decision.** In a second Xbox One recording both prompts are still visible at 0:30. At 0:31 their space says Loading and a white skull appears near the corner. At 0:32 strokes radiate from the skull. At 0:33 the title artwork gives way to a black sheet carrying one tip, with the animated mark still near the corner. Different tips are visible at 0:40, 0:43 and 0:48. The recorder identifies A as the input for advancing them. At 0:54 the city is visible and at 0:58 the player and HUD are on screen. The footage shows the stages of a real load on this machine. [The Games Trunk’s Xbox One capture, 0:30–0:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z9kdUe9YRyI&t=30s)

**Apply it to a gig-poster archive.** Give a returning visitor two inked buttons, “Open my saved set” and “Choose another set”. New visitors only need “Browse posters”. Let the solid ink in the focused button reverse colour, with a visible keyboard focus ring. Keep the poster in place on activation. If the saved set still needs fetching, change that button to “Opening set”, announce the state once and offer a retry on failure. When the contact sheet arrives, move focus to its heading. Back restores the previous poster. A static print and an immediate change of state carry the same flow on touch or with reduced motion. Challenge the two-button design if the site has no saved sets.

**How to read the evidence.** These playheads were inspected as decoded frames from recordings of the games. A change of focus, a visible press prompt and a completed transition are distinct observations. Console glyphs identify offered inputs, and a pulsing background can continue with nobody touching the controls. Exact controller events, pointer hover and easing curves would need their own evidence. The website examples describe proposed interactions. Keep each action in a styled container with a single readable label, give keyboard focus equal standing with pointer focus, and make a first tap work.
