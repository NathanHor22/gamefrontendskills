# How the Arkham menus move

The four PC games give the player different things to read. In *Asylum* the current word changes on a sheet. In *City* a compact icon acquires its full name as you explore. In *Origins* the same row stays selected through a change of shot. In *Knight* a save slot tells you which game you are about to resume.

## Arkham Asylum

**Title to profile.** The moon fills the left side, Batman stands on the right, and a single start prompt sits below the title. At 0:07.8 the title letters spread apart. At 0:08 they are faint and the smaller prompt has cleared. At 0:08.4 the moonlit scene stands alone. The stacked save sheets are visible at 0:10. That short interval gives the outgoing title and incoming choices distinct entrances on the same stage. The PC manual identifies four player profiles and separates choosing a profile from entering Story or Challenge Mode. [Games Interface PC capture, 0:06–0:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ykB9BN9Uc8I&t=6s) · [Publisher’s PC manual, printed p. 5](https://support.na.square-enix.com/document/manual/985/Batman_Arkham_Asylum_PC_Manual.pdf#page=4)

**Profile to main menu.** In a second PC recording marked v1.1, two lower save sheets are still visible at 0:01.2. At 0:01.4 only Batman’s chest remains. The new menu is partially drawn at 0:01.6 and legible at 0:01.8. Behind it the chest darkens, then a moonlit profile resolves by 0:03.4. The controls finish arriving before the change of portrait. [Interface Love’s runtime capture, 0:01–0:04](https://ilikeinterfaces.com/2015/03/29/main-menu-ui-batman-arkham-asylum/)

**Changing a row.** Condensed white capitals share one irregular black sheet. The current word turns gold and gains a gold marker at its left. At 0:03.4 the pointer rests over Continue Story. It remains there at 0:04.4 and 0:05 while Downloadable Content and Character Bios take the highlight. The recording establishes selection feedback. That parked pointer is a reason to leave the mouse-hover gesture unassigned. At 0:12.6 Exit to Desktop is still highlighted. At 0:12.8 a smaller decision sheet has appeared over a dimmed menu. This is a second decision with its own current answer. [Interface Love’s capture, 0:03–0:05 and 0:12–0:15](https://ilikeinterfaces.com/2015/03/29/main-menu-ui-batman-arkham-asylum/)

## Arkham City

**Explore without entering.** A grid of square pictograms holds its position below one bold action name. At 0:20.4 the first tile identifies Continue Story. At 0:20.6 the neighbouring question-mark tile fills with light and its heading already reads Riddler’s Revenge. At 0:20.8 the flash has receded to a bright border and glyph and the explanation is legible. At 0:21.4 the statistics tile is brightening. At 0:22 a pointer is visible inside the flashing Story Synopsis tile. This is pointer-correlated feedback across several destinations on one screen. The footage does not record mouse-button events. [Games Interface PC capture, 0:20–0:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qOYl2ZU4Ww8&t=20s)

**Enter the destination.** Riddler’s Revenge is current at 0:29. At 0:30 the grid has cleared and the illustrated Batman remains. The image is black at 0:31–0:32, a small bat appears at 0:33, and a green-lit character roster occupies the frame at 0:34. A bright tile answers “which option?” immediately. The replacement roster answers “what can I do here?” after commitment. The observed wait belongs to this recording and machine. [Same PC capture, 0:29–0:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qOYl2ZU4Ww8&t=29s)

## Arkham Origins

**Keep the choice still through an active backdrop.** At 0:26 Story is selected in a long cyan row against a mostly monochrome composition with Batman and a red diagonal accent. At 0:28 the shot has changed to a close encounter, but Story and its band still occupy the same menu position. By 0:30 the band has moved down to Exit. At 0:31 a translucent confirmation sheet occupies the list area and No is bright. At 0:33 the menu is back with Exit still current. A change of shot alone is unreliable evidence of the player changing focus. The manual also pictures the cyan menu band in the shipped PC interface. [Games Interface PC capture, 0:26–0:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ddef0KIPnwY&t=26s) · [PC manual, printed pp. 13–14](https://shared.akamai.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/209000/manuals/BAO_PC_Manual_Eng_120513.pdf#page=14)

## Arkham Knight

**Name the work between screens.** A rim-lit, forward-facing cowl leaves the title and start prompt clear on the left. At 0:39 the occupied save slot carries a location, progress and difficulty. Three slots underneath are empty. Between 0:40 and 0:43 a centred status reports a downloadable-content check. By 0:43.7 it has cleared, exposing a side-on view of Batman. At 0:44 the menu heading is appearing and at 0:44.4 the list and Continue Story highlight are visible. The selected row has a cyan glow and the saved percentage. The PC manual documents clicking the title or pressing Enter, then choosing a save. The waiting state in this capture is evidence for a named operation, never a target duration for a website. [JooshyFruit’s PC capture, 0:35–0:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vQrh92Xmwt0&t=35s) · [PC manual, printed pp. 6 and 9–10](https://steamcdn-a.akamaihd.net/steam/apps/208650/manuals/BAK_PC_ManInt_ENG_072015_v22_MC.pdf#page=4)

## Handheld and headset editions

**Arkham Origins Blackgate, PlayStation Vita.** At 0:34 an explicit touchscreen invitation fills a broad white strip over a portrait divided into vertical panels. At 0:37 three save slots run across the bottom. The occupied slot previews the Lighthouse, play time and completion above it. At 0:39 the empty slot has a red outline and its large preview is also empty. By 0:41 the main menu is back on the portrait with New Game in a white band. At 0:44 the controls clear, at 0:46 bat silhouettes cross the image, and at 0:48 only a small bat remains against black. For a phone-sized sketchbook archive, put thumbnails within thumb reach and give the selected book a generous preview. An empty book gets its own clear state. Rebecca Wallace, who designed and implemented the handheld UI, shows distinct Vita and 3DS layouts in her portfolio. These playheads cover Vita. [Placlutwo’s Vita recording, 0:34–0:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_oht10hgDSQ&t=34s) · [Wallace’s design and screens](https://rekaraccoon.artstation.com/projects/kN8koz)

**Arkham VR, PlayStation VR.** The title occupies a place on a Gotham rooftop. At 0:30 the wordmark is straight ahead. Looking up or aside takes it partly or completely out of view. At 1:00 the player faces it and its press prompt again. At 1:10 the skyline is visible with the wordmark cleared, followed by black and the first story scene. A second PSVR recording shows an Act 1, Origin choice and Options suspended in that same skyline. Looking around changes the viewpoint while leaving the entry where the player found it. In a virtual exhibition, an exhibit label can belong to the wall beside its work. Give a flat-screen visitor an equally direct catalogue button and an ordinary Back path. [Batman Arkham Videos’ PSVR capture, 0:30–1:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=g0zBx1KiUU0&t=30s) · [GameRiot’s PSVR chapter menu, 1:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ujgB5iooErg&t=112s)

**Arkham Shadow, Meta Quest.** This entry uses a physical-looking console. At 0:03 its luminous bat mark fills the monitor, and bats cross it at 0:07. By 0:11 the monitor presents three difficulty portraits, each with a readable name. A pointer ray crosses from the centre portrait at 0:14 to the right portrait at 0:15. At 0:16 a new screen has one Begin New Game button. At 0:18 the ray ends on its bright cyan fill. By 0:20 the console is dimming and at 0:22 the frame is black. A second recording shows the same choices from a different head angle. On a public site, a display embedded in a workshop scene can keep its physical frame as choices change. Use named HTML buttons for the choices, let keyboard and pointer highlight the same surface, and open it on the first tap. [Batman Arkham Videos’ Quest recording, 0:03–0:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Xy10ZhiEsK8&t=3s) · [ThisKory’s difficulty selection, 0:09–0:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=va_bBXtYvLU&t=9s)

## Website transfer

For a photographic contact-sheet archive, mount each shoot name on a flat photographic sleeve. Let pointer intent or keyboard focus expose its first frame while the other sleeves stay within reach. Changing focus mid-reveal immediately retargets the preview. Activating a sleeve opens the complete shoot and moves focus to its title. Back restores the sleeve and its place in the list. On touch, the first tap opens it directly. Use a still for reduced motion and show a loading message only when the full set is actually being fetched. The stable index comes from *City*, the independent arrival of the controls and artwork from *Asylum*, and the distinction between real waiting and a visual handoff from *Knight*.

**Inspection scope.** These playheads were checked against rendered video frames on 20 November 2026. They identify visible states and their order. The captures supply no physical input log, easing curve or universal frame duration. The four PC studies cover the original releases. The *Return to Arkham* remasters need separate evidence.
