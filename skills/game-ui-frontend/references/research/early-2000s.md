# Early-2000s games as art direction

Pumpo’s *The Timeless Aesthetics Of Early 2000's Games* ranges from beachside fighters to cold console imagery and illustrated city streets. For a brief that says “early 2000s,” identify which moment feels right for this audience. **How the identity was made.** Director Masayoshi Kikuchi describes choosing cel shading to carry Ryuta Ueda’s drawings into 3D. Ueda describes outlines, two-tone shading, and restricting background colours like print inks. Kikuchi recalls composer Hideki Naganuma reading the entire design specification. Pick a drawing or object that already expresses the project and make the rendering, lettering and sound answer to it. [Interview with Kikuchi and Ueda](https://www.4gamer.net/games/999/G999903/20240307087/)

## Original direction for a listener-supported radio station

“Help listeners find a show they love and play the latest episode.” Take the station’s own gig flyers as the source material. Draw its presenters in two ink colours against flat shadows, and letter each show name to fit the shape of its flyer. Keep the broadcast time and Play button in consistent positions. At rest, the show name, portrait and next broadcast answer the first decision. Opening the flyer reveals the episode, guests and track list.

On focus or pointer entry, a second-ink edge appears beyond the flyer and a corner lifts. On exit it folds back into the stack. Selecting a show pins that flyer while the previous one slips behind it. Sound begins on **Play episode**. Browsing other shows leaves the current programme playing and its title visible. On a phone, stack the flyers in schedule order with tap-to-open titles. Reduced motion uses the ink edge and selected title immediately. This choreography belongs to the station.
