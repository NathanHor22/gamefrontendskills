For a brief that says “early 2000s,” identify which moment feels right for this audience. ## Follow the essay from hardware to handling

**Why these games shared a moment.** The opening console reveal gives way to games from several genres, hidden roster silhouettes and the author's memories of unlocking things through play. His Jill Valentine and Lara Croft comparisons give an artist a more useful question than a generation label. Which expressive features survive the increase in detail? The author then connects fifth-generation experiments with controls, voice and models to what later developers could build upon. He also credits lower production costs with allowing risk. That last point is his explanation for the range on screen. The montage supplies no production budgets. For a studio archive, display two comparable project images at the same scale so visitors can discover a change in expression for themselves. ## Original direction for a listener-supported radio station

“Help listeners find a show they love and play the latest episode.” Take the station’s own gig flyers as the source material. Draw its presenters in two ink colours against flat shadows, and letter each show name to fit the shape of its flyer. Keep the broadcast time and Play button in consistent positions. At rest, the show name, portrait and next broadcast answer the first decision. Opening the flyer reveals the episode, guests and track list.

On focus or pointer entry, a second-ink edge appears beyond the flyer and a corner lifts. On exit it folds back into the stack. Selecting a show pins that flyer while the previous one slips behind it. Sound begins on **Play episode**. Browsing other shows leaves the current programme playing and its title visible. On a phone, stack the flyers in schedule order with tap-to-open titles. Reduced motion uses the ink edge and selected title immediately. This choreography belongs to the station.
