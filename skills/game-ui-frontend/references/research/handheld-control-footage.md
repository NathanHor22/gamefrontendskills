# Controls across 150 PSP games

[Game Box2002’s 15-minute PSP compilation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AgKACS46XII) puts one game on screen about every six seconds. This guide follows the first 100 in upload order. [Continue with the final 50 games](handheld-control-footage-final.md). The links use the uploader’s chapter times. For each entry we compared three frames from the original recording at approximately half a second, two and a half seconds and four and a half seconds after that time. They show gameplay, and occasionally a command or contextual prompt. They rarely show a complete menu interaction. The cover, year, file size and the hands on a blue controller belong to the edit.

Each row pairs visible evidence with something new to try on the web. A moving marker shows *where the game locates selection*. It cannot establish a mouse-hover curve, an exact pulse period or the original input. In particular the two Spider-Man chapters are the PSP editions of *Spider-Man 3* and *Spider-Man 2*, and the Persona chapter shows a battle in *Persona 3 Portable*.
