# When a title scene becomes the first action

God of War lets the player spend time with a character before the story begins. Crash Bandicoot lets a character perform while the player finds the controls. Those approaches suggest two different questions for a website. What will the visitor learn by watching the scene, and can they act the moment they recognise what they want?

The 2005 and 2018 games reveal different things on activation. In the original, the player's last choice is difficulty. The close portrait opens into a wide shot that finally shows where Kratos stands. In 2018 the yellow handprint is visible before the choice. Waiting keeps its meaning open. Starting gives it a specific purpose when Kratos reaches up and touches it. Santa Monica Studio describes the broader creative commitment to a continuous camera. The frames above show how the tree works within this particular opening. [Santa Monica Studio on its camera](https://blog.playstation.com/archive/2018/09/28/3-key-scenes-from-god-of-war-that-helped-define-its-incredible-cinematic-style)

**A portfolio application.** A ceramicist’s kiln can already be visible beside a solid clay-coloured button labelled **Open the kiln**. Put hover and keyboard focus on the button face. On activation, let the door open onto the finished pots. Give each pot a link immediately and let Back return to the same piece. If the door animation is disabled or interrupted, opening the kiln still reveals the collection. A second gateway between the open door and the pots would spend the gesture twice.

The PSP recordings reveal an important difference between **browsing** and **leaving**. On Chains of Olympus, visiting Options keeps the title composition available when the player returns. Starting the story moves the camera into the eye. Ghost of Sparta spends its large change of brightness on entry into the story. Leave the rest of the site still when attention simply moves from one button to the next. Give activation an observable result even for someone who sees only the final frame.

The 1996 game gets the order right for a visitor in a hurry. The list is present while the mascot has more to do. A returning visitor knows where to go without waiting for the pose. The trilogy solves a different problem. Three games share one opening, and growing one emblem makes the scope of its Continue or New Game action clear. The developer also describes its effort to unify menus and saves across the collection. [Vicarious Visions on rebuilding the trilogy](https://blog.playstation.com/2016/12/03/crash-bandicoot-n-sane-trilogy-first-screens-in-depth-details/)

**A record-label application.** Put three illustrated record sleeves in the same listening room. On keyboard focus or fine-pointer hover, bring the chosen sleeve forward a little and keep its single readable label inside its button. Activation opens that release and its tracks. A first tap goes straight to the release. Beside each track, show a Play control only when audio is available. A visitor who tabs across all three sleeves should hear silence until they choose to listen.

Crash Tag Team Racing makes hierarchy tangible with a signpost, a set of destinations, then a confirmation above the board. LocoRoco has room for several moving characters because the action keeps the same small area of screen. On a phone, keep a band's cover artwork animated within its own space and give the band a stationary, thumb-sized button. A label that moves away from a finger would defeat both approaches. The PSP Gran Turismo recording adds a contrasting example of a compact object grid. [Gran Turismo menu study](gran-turismo-menus.md)

**How to read the footage.** The cited times are playheads in the upload. The God of War and Crash comparison videos are edited. The title identifies God of War III Remastered. Other multiplatform chapters do not establish the capture hardware or build. The separate handheld recordings identify the PSP editions. Crash Tag Team Racing’s recorder specifically names PPSSPP. A brighter word or plate establishes the currently selected item. Pointer movement, switch depression, input latency and easing curves would need separate evidence. For a directly visible PC pointer, see the [Spider-Man menu study](spider-man-title.md).
