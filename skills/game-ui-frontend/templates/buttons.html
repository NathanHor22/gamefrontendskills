Button archetypes

1 · Breakout primary

The single biggest control on the view. An emblem sits outside the plate and lifts on intent; a contour rail draws outside the edge. On leave the emblem drops first and the rail retracts after it.

2 · Signal band text action

No box at all. The label barely changes; a blurred field of colour grows outward from behind it, far past the text. Use for title-screen-style menus with few choices.

3 · Keycap secondary actions

Quiet secondary actions whose keyboard shortcut hangs below the plaque. The shortcut is real (press S or B) and declared with aria-keyshortcuts.

4 · Hold to confirm

Destructive actions borrow the game "hold" gesture. Holding fills the plate; releasing early drains it from where it was. People who cannot hold can click twice instead.

Press and hold, or click twice, to delete.
